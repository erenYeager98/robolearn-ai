# 📘 User Manual

Welcome to your personalized study companion!  
This manual will guide you step by step on how to use the app effectively to get the most out of your study sessions.  

---

## 🏠 Home Page  

<img width="1918" height="904" alt="home" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3b0a295e-b2f7-4374-9c8d-8536e2c67163" />

When you open the app, you’ll arrive at the **Home Page**. Here, you can begin your learning journey:  

- **Search Bar** → Type your query directly.  
- **Voice Input** → Use the microphone button for voice transcription to input your question hands-free.  
- **Start Studying** → After entering your query, select your **preferred study time** to structure your session.  

---

## ⏳ Choose Study Time  

<img width="1919" height="916" alt="choose_model" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d2f3108b-76ba-4a2a-84d6-d07a42ce7f6c" />

This step helps you manage your focus:  

1. Select the **duration** you’d like to dedicate to studying (short, medium, or long sessions).  
2. The app adapts the amount of content delivered to fit within your chosen time frame.  

---

## 📚 Select Learning Material  

You’ll then be given options to decide **what type of study resources** you want to use:  

- **Locally Curated Study Materials** → Content chosen and organized by educators or your institution.  
- **Global Study Materials** → A wider pool of resources gathered from global databases.  
- **Learn by Topic** → If you prefer a structured, topic-wise learning approach, this option guides you through materials in a logical order.  

---

## 🎓 Learning Interface  

<img width="1919" height="910" alt="learn" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ed2a12eb-a7f7-4305-bd6d-8dcefd518dc2" />

Once your materials are loaded, you’ll see:  

- **Main Learning Content** → Clean, readable study materials.  
- **Narrative Learning Mode** → Step-by-step explanations for better understanding.  
- **Research Papers Section** → Related academic research papers displayed alongside your materials for deeper exploration.  

---

## 📑 Browse & Learn  

<img width="1917" height="912" alt="ppt" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a9be58f2-97e8-47ef-ae62-ecf963a009c4" />

Navigate through your content with ease:  

- **Topic Navigation** → Quickly jump between subtopics within the same subject.  
- **PPT Mode** → Learn through summarized slides for a more visual, concise understanding.  
- **Continuous Learning Flow** → The app remembers your progress so you can return later without losing track.  

---

## ✅ Tips for Best Use  

- Use the **voice input** for faster queries if you don’t want to type.  
- Choose shorter sessions if you’re revising, and longer ones if you’re learning a new concept.  
- Explore both **local** and **global** materials for a well-rounded understanding.  
- Dive into the **research papers** if you want advanced, reference-level insights.
